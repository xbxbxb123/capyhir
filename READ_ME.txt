To turn the DETAILED_RACE_TEMPLATE_SETUP.txt into a batch file, make sure you have "file name extensions" turned on for your file explorer. Then, right click DETAILED_RACE_TEMPLATE_SETUP.txt, click "Rename", and replace ".txt" with ".bat" and press enter. You'll get a little warning, but just ignore it and hit "YES".

Now double click to run it.

Feel free to delete any files you don't want for your race! Just remember to delete ANY associated files! For examples, humanoid fossils have objects, items, and patch a treasure pool.
